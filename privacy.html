LASTCalc — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

LASTCalc is a local anaesthetic dosing tool published by Periop Digital ("we"). It has no accounts, no analytics and no tracking, and no servers that store your information. This policy explains what does and does not leave your device.

Clinical data you enter

Weights, heights, ages, drugs and volumes you enter are processed on your device to produce calculations. They are not transmitted to us or to anyone else. LASTCalc is not designed to store patient-identifiable information; please do not enter any.

Settings stored on your device

The app stores a few small preferences (for example that you have accepted the disclaimer) in your device's local storage. You can clear these by clearing the app's data.

Camera (optional scan feature)

If you use the optional record-scanning feature, the app asks for camera access. Text recognition runs on your device; the image is not saved or sent anywhere and is discarded once recognition completes.

Third-party resources

To display the app, your device may load fonts from Google Fonts and open-source code libraries from the jsDelivr content network. These providers may see your IP address as part of delivering those files, under their own privacy policies.

Exports

Data leaves your device only if you choose to print, save a PDF/PNG, or share a report, to a destination you select.

Children

LASTCalc is intended for healthcare professionals and is not directed at children.

Not a medical device

LASTCalc is a decision-support aid and not a substitute for clinical judgement or local protocol. See the in-app disclaimer.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by the date above.
